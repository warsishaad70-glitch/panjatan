Welcome to a Living Testament of Love

Darbar-e-Panjatan Paak Trust is the heart of Hazaribagh’s spiritual and social conscience — a living testament to the eternal love for Muhammadﷺ and his Holy Household (Ahle Bait). We are a community of hearts united in devotion, translating the supreme values of the Panjatan Paak into tangible acts of compassion, justice, and enlightenment.

Here, faith is alive. It feeds the hungry, heals the sick, educates the curious, and comforts the weary. Every action is an offering (Nazrana) at the sacred threshold.

Our Spiritual Lineage: Connected to the ethos of Sayyed Haji Waris Ali Shah (R.A.) of the Warsiyya tradition, we propagate the message of love for Ahl-e-Bait through service to all of humanity. To join this Darbar is to become part of this sacred spiritual connection (Nisbat).

True Ishq-e-Ahle Bait is proven not only in mourning but in building; not only in remembrance but in action. Darbar-e-Panjatan Paak is where spiritual reverence meets social revolution.

OUR SPIRITUAL COMPASS

The Five Luminaries: Our Eternal Guides

They are the Ark of Salvation, our spiritual foundation, and the embodiment of the divine values we strive to live by.

Prophet Muhammadﷺ – Mercy for all worlds
Imam Ali A.S. – Gate of Knowledge, Lion of Justice
Sayyida Fatima Zahra S.A. – Radiant, Purity, Grace
Imam Hasan A.S. – Noble Peacemaker
Imam Hussain A.S. – Master of Martyrs, Truth against oppression

Their collective legacy is our roadmap: ‘Adl (Justice), ‘Ilm (Knowledge), Sabr (Patience), Shuja’at (Courage), and Rahma (Mercy).

OUR VISION & MISSION

To cultivate a community that mirrors the noble character (Akhlaq) of the Panjatan Paak.

Our Mission

  • Ignite Hearts with sincere love for Ahle Bait.
  • Embody Teachings: justice of Ali, patience of Fatima, peace of Hasan, sacrifice of Hussain.
  • Serve Unconditionally with enduring humanitarian projects.
  • Bridge Divides: interfaith dialogue, social harmony.
  • Empower Generations: nurture youth with spiritual strength.
